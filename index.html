Hey there!

Videographer & Documentary Filmmaker

I'm a freelance videographer based in Caithness, North Scotland. Originally from the Clwydian hills of North Wales, I spend my time exploring forests and sketching landscapes.

My commercial work focuses on the outdoor, lifestyle, tourism and wellbeing sectors. With a background in anthropology & journalism, my projects explore the impacts made on local culture and the surrounding environment, while fostering collaborations between organisations. Alongside freelancing, I work regularly with Sidh C Art to produce ongoing marketing content.
