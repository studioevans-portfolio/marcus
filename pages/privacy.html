Privacy Policy

Information I collect

This website does not use contact forms, user accounts, analytics, advertising trackers or other tools designed to collect personal information from visitors.

The website includes a contact button which allows you to contact me by email. When you choose to do so, your email application will open and any personal information you provide will be handled through your email service.

Embedded YouTube videos

This website contains videos embedded from YouTube. When you view or interact with an embedded YouTube video, YouTube and Google may collect certain information about your device or your interaction with the video, and may use cookies or similar technologies. This is controlled by YouTube and Google rather than by this website.

For more information, please refer to Google's privacy policy and YouTube's terms and privacy information.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use its own analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party services, such as YouTube, may use cookies or similar technologies when their content is embedded on the website.

Information you provide by email

If you choose to contact me by email, I will use the information you provide to respond to your message and, where relevant, discuss or carry out a project with you.

I will not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights in relation to your personal information, including the right to request access to, correction or deletion of information I hold about you, where applicable.

You can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you have concerns about how your personal information is handled.

Email Communications

When you contact Marcus Wyn by email, we may process your name, email address, the contents of your correspondence and any other information you choose to provide.

We use third-party email delivery services to send emails from our business email address. These services may process limited technical information required to deliver and secure email communications.

We do not use ordinary one-to-one email correspondence for marketing purposes, and we do not use tracking pixels, open tracking or click tracking in these emails. We do not use these emails to monitor whether a recipient has opened an email, clicked a link or otherwise interacted with its contents.

Email correspondence may be retained where reasonably necessary for providing our services, maintaining business records, resolving enquiries or meeting legal and contractual obligations.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
